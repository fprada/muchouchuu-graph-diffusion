### Current scientific conclusion

- Transport homogeneity is statistically consistent with no evolution
  over 0 <= z <= 2.03.
- Density homogeneity is consistent with no evolution through z~1.
- At z=2.03 the density scale shows a suggestive decrease relative to
  z=0, with P(delta R < 0) = 0.94352, but the 95% interval includes zero.
